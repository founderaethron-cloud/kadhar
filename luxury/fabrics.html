The fabric library

Cloth first

We don’t name garments by fabric composition. We build fabric platforms, each engineered for a purpose, and design garments around them.

Platforms in development

Seven cloths, one foundation.

KH-01Handwoven cottonLuxury everyday. The foundation cloth.
KH-02Handwoven cotton + hempStructure and strength for outerwear and utility.
KH-03Handwoven cotton + TENCEL™ LyocellFluid drape for relaxed, oversized pieces.
KH-04Handwoven cotton + linenCool and breathable for summer.
KH-05Handwoven cotton + silkA luxury capsule cloth.
KH-06Handwoven cotton + bambooUltra-soft, with moisture management.
KH-07Travel weaveLighter, wrinkle-resistant, made to pack.

Each platform is sampled, wear-tested and wash-tested before it reaches a garment.

How a garment is engineered

Three layers of craft.

01

The fibre

Handspun cotton, blended with hemp, linen, lyocell or bamboo for strength, cool or drape.

02

The fabric

Weave, weight and finishing: enzyme softening, pre-shrinking, natural dyes.

03

The garment

Shoulder slope, collar roll, stitch density, shrinkage: the details you feel before you notice.
